# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single experienced recreational lifter using a phone between sets. They are focused, physically occupied, often holding the device one-handed, and want to record weight and repetitions without interrupting training.

## Product Purpose

Liftd is a mobile-only progressive-overload logbook. It makes logging a set fast, keeps previous performance visible, and turns workout history into clear evidence of strength progress. Success means a lifter can record a set in seconds and immediately understand what to attempt next.

## Brand Personality

Precise, serious, quiet. Liftd should feel like a dependable training instrument rather than a social fitness feed, wellness coach, or gamified habit app.

## Anti-references

- Repetitive dark cards with identical visual weight.
- Emoji as the primary exercise identity system.
- Wellness dashboards, streak pressure, recovery scores, community feeds, and coaching features.
- Cinematic workout flows that slow repeated set entry.
- Decorative neon, glass effects, oversized radii, and colour without functional meaning.

## Design Principles

- The next action is always reachable with one thumb.
- Previous performance stays beside the values being entered.
- Density is useful when alignment and hierarchy remain precise.
- Progress is expressed as one clear story, not a grid of competing metrics.
- The interface disappears during training and becomes analytical afterward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast. Preserve browser zoom, use 44px minimum touch targets, support reduced motion, expose visible focus states, and never communicate completion, error, or progress through colour alone.
